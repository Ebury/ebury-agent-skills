# Note to a supplier about a payment

Adapt the wording to the user's language and tone. Keep the facts exactly as Ebury returned them.

## Payment sent

> Hi {supplier contact},
>
> We sent payment for {invoice/reference} on {payment date}:
>
> - Amount: {amount} {currency}
> - Reference: {payment reference}
> - Sent to the account ending {last 4 characters}
> - Tracking id: {uuid} (only if available)
>
> International payments can take a few business days to arrive. If you can't see it, your bank can trace it with the tracking id and reference above.
>
> Thanks,
> {user's name}

## Payment in progress

> Hi {supplier contact},
>
> Payment for {invoice/reference}, {amount} {currency}, is in progress on our side and scheduled for {payment date}. We'll confirm once it has been sent.
>
> Thanks,
> {user's name}

Don't include: full account numbers, internal ids other than the payment reference and tracking id, or the reason for any hold or check.
